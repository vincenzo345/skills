Skill summary and workflow context

Foundation: setup-repo, then meeting-based discovery through to-record and to-scope.

Clarify: grill-with-docs. Large uncertainty: wayfinder. Incoming work: triage.

Design: design with focused prototype evidence as needed.

Plan: to-spec → to-tickets.

Build: implement per work item, or implement-spec for coordinated execution. Test-first development supports implementation.

Review and learn: code-review, PR preparation, then retro for session learning.

Workflow context: guide-me. The proposed broader lifecycle identifies readiness, architecture-view maintenance, and release-verification gaps.

Scope: how the repository's engineering skills guide software work, rather than how skill packages are published. The eight phases are an executive synthesis of current instructions, not eight mandatory gates. The meeting-based discovery route applies when meeting evidence is the source of business requirements. Initial repository setup configures tracking and domain documentation. This view describes prescribed behavior; it does not assert measured adoption, productivity gains, or production readiness. All explanations and interactions are included in this file; no repository access or internet connection is required.
