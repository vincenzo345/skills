# Synthetic interview

**Coordinator:** A request arrives by email. I log it in the case tracker and check whether the vendor tax form is present.

**Coordinator:** If the form is missing, I email the vendor and the case waits. Nobody owns reminders consistently.

**Coordinator:** Requests of at least $10,000 go to Finance. Smaller requests go straight to the department approver.

**Finance analyst:** I compare the amount with the contract. Sometimes Operations changes the amount after I approve it.

**Operations clerk:** Once approval is complete, I enter the payment manually in the ERP and email the requester.

**Builder:** We want to reduce the time from receipt to approved payment without removing the financial control.
