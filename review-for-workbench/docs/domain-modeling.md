## What it does

`domain-modeling` sharpens canonical terms in `CONTEXT.md` and records rare, durable trade-offs as ADRs when writes are authorized. Analysis-only requests remain read-only.

Evidence-backed facts may enter the glossary. An accepted ADR choice must be confirmed by its owner or explicitly delegated within recorded bounds; evidence may inform the choice but cannot provide authority for it. Proposals and evidence blockers remain provisional, with decision owner and source provenance available in ADR metadata.
