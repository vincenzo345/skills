---
name: grill-with-docs
description: A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design, retaining requirements in the effort and qualifying vocabulary and decisions in project domain docs.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

1. Read the configured tracker and [shared work-record contract](../setup-repo/WORK-RECORDS.md). Resume the identified effort; create one stable request-named effort only for a new request. Read its discovery record and current decision when present before asking again. For local writes, use [local planning operations](../wayfinder/LOCAL-PLANNING.md); remote decision answers remain on the configured tracker.
2. Call the Skill tool twice, for "grilling" and "domain-modeling". Preserve the interview's rounds and human judgment. A reply continues the unresolved question; record missing human choices as open questions until answered.
3. Persist ordinary requirements, findings, constraints, and open questions in the effort's discovery record as they become clear. When working a Wayfinder decision, keep its authoritative answer in that decision and link to it from discovery instead of copying it. Include the actual supplied answer or a faithful concise account; never manufacture approval. Shared terms still belong in the configured glossary, and only qualifying decisions become project ADRs.
4. At the boundary, link the effort and durable findings, identify unresolved input, and name the next invocation. Carry those same references into to-spec. A resolved interview does not approve an implementation breakdown or begin implementation.
